## Evidence quality note

Evidence 01 is direct executed software evidence with retained raw outputs and environment metadata. Confidence is limited by self-authored scenarios, one machine/platform, synthetic fixtures, schema creation outside Alembic, unpinned repository dependencies, and absence of independently specified clinical cases.

Evidence 02 is direct deterministic function-level evidence with frozen cases and complete raw outputs. It measures enforcement coverage, not live-model defect frequency or clinical correctness. The failed result must narrow the explanation claims even though the separate finding-authority boundary remains supported.

Evidence 03 strongly establishes source-file identity but not transformation lineage. Evidence 04 is direct executed operational evidence with all measurements retained, but its synthetic single-machine warm-cache design is deliberately narrower than production performance or clinical validation. Evidence 05 directly distinguishes the selected persistence that works from five semantics that prevent a broad audit-trail claim. Evidence 06 adds the first independently specified terminology-conformance axis and exposes material normalization limitations; its purposive case rate is not a population estimate. Evidence 07 is a transparent targeted review rather than a systematic review; it is adequate to remove broad novelty language but cannot prove that no closer prior implementation exists.
