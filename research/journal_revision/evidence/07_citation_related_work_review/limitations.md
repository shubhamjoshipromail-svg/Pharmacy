# Limitations

1. This was a targeted citation and related-work review, not a systematic or scoping review. It did not use a registered search strategy, duplicate screening, formal risk-of-bias assessment, citation-network search, or exhaustive database coverage.
2. The search emphasized English-language primary, publisher, and official sources that were accessible on 2026-07-14. Relevant paywalled, non-English, unpublished, or differently indexed work may be missing.
3. Website and guidance content is mutable. The inventory records checked URLs and dates but does not archive copyrighted full text.
4. Crossref was used as a metadata cross-check, not as the sole authority. The Hevner page-range discrepancy illustrates why the publisher artifact was preferred.
5. The 2026 hybrid DDI source is an SSRN preprint. Its high-level overlap is relevant to novelty, but its methods and claims were not independently validated and should not be treated as clinical evidence.
6. The comparison is at the method-class and reported-evidence level. It does not reproduce other systems, compare code, or establish patent novelty.
7. No librarian, pharmacist, regulatory counsel, or second reviewer independently screened or adjudicated this review.
8. FDA and licensing sources support contextual statements only. This review is not legal advice, a regulatory classification, or a license determination.
9. Citation accuracy cannot repair unsupported project claims. Evidence 02, 03, 05, and 06 still require negative or qualified reporting regardless of the quality of the citations.

## Residual risk

A target journal may require a more formal literature-search method or newer sources at submission time. The author should rerun a journal-specific literature update immediately before submission and obtain domain review of the final related-work section.
