## Comparative judgment

The exact combination of implementation details is not the appropriate novelty standard. Each constituent idea—and even the high-level rule-based-plus-LLM combination—has precedent. The publishable value lies in a transparent case study that makes the authority allocation executable and then reports where the implementation succeeds and fails.

The closest missing comparison is not another large model. It is a deterministic, source-filled template rendered from the identical structured finding. Until that baseline and pharmacist evaluation exist, the generative component should be described as optional and unevaluated, not beneficial.
